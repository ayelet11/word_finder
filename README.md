Some elderly people or people who suffered strokes have difficulty finding the specific word to communicate their needs and desires. The condition is called aphasia.
This app stores words of a personalized with their description, category and tags in order to help them or their caretaker find the word by its context and/or description in real time. 
This application is based on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)  to help the adults with aphasia retrieve words from their personalized vocabulary store. 
It uses LangChain, ChromaDB, and OpenAI API.
